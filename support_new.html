🏥 About Dr Bèo

Dr Bèo is a comprehensive medical reference application designed specifically for healthcare professionals. Our app provides quick access to essential medical scoring systems and clinical tools to support evidence-based medical practice.

🎯 SOFA Score Calculator

Sequential Organ Failure Assessment scoring system for ICU patients with customizable unit conversions.

🔧 User-Friendly Interface

Modern, intuitive design optimized for quick calculations during clinical practice.

🌐 Multi-language Support

Available in multiple languages to serve healthcare professionals worldwide.

📱 Offline Access

All tools work offline, ensuring reliable access in any clinical environment.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the SOFA Score calculator?

Navigate to the SOFA Score section and input the required patient parameters. The app will automatically calculate the score and provide interpretation guidance.

Can I use this app for patient care decisions?

Dr Bèo is a reference tool to support clinical decision-making. Always use your clinical judgment and consult with colleagues when making patient care decisions.

Is my data secure?

Yes, Dr Bèo operates entirely offline and does not collect, store, or transmit any patient data. Your privacy and patient confidentiality are fully protected.

How often is the app updated?

We regularly update Dr Bèo to improve functionality and add new features based on user feedback and medical best practices.

🆘 Technical Support

  • App crashes or won't start: Try restarting your device and ensure you have the latest version installed.
  • Calculation issues: Verify all input values are within normal physiological ranges.
  • Display problems: Check your device's accessibility settings and font size preferences.
  • Other issues: Contact us at support@drbeo.com with a detailed description.
