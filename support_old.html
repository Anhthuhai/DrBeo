📱 About DR-ICU

DR-ICU: Emergency Medical Protocols for Critical Care is a comprehensive medical reference application designed for healthcare professionals working in intensive care units and emergency medicine.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Who can use this app?

A: DR-ICU is designed for qualified healthcare professionals including emergency medicine physicians, intensivists, critical care specialists, ICU/Emergency nurses, medical students, residents, and emergency medical technicians.

Q: Is this app a substitute for medical training?

A: No, DR-ICU is a reference tool only. It should be used by qualified professionals and never replace proper medical training, clinical judgment, or established medical procedures.

Q: How do I report bugs or suggest features?

A: Please email us at support.dricu@gmail.com with detailed information about the issue or suggestion.

Q: Is my data secure?

A: Yes, DR-ICU does not collect or transmit any personal data. All information is stored locally on your device.
